BRASSLINE AUDIO UPDATE - SOURCE CREDITS

Gunshot recordings: The Free Firearm Sound Library
Recorded by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras and Matthew Nanney.
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public-domain dedication).
https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Archive: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Prepared%20SFX%20Library.7z
Selected recordings: AK-47/C_28P.wav, 1911/A_42P.wav, Tikka/W_29P.wav.
Modified for BRASSLINE: individual shot selection, attack trimming, mono
conversion for positional playback, filtering, low-body layer, level matching,
tail trimming and Ogg Vorbis encoding. No recordings from commercial games.

Foley/impact recordings: Impact Sounds (1.0), Kenney (www.kenney.nl), 2019.
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public-domain dedication).
https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Modified for BRASSLINE: cutting, filtering, layering, timing, resampling and
level matching. Used in movement, equipment, impacts, parry and hit feedback.
Individual source/take information is in provenance.json beside this file.

Original BRASSLINE sound design: filtered blast/air layers, sword swish,
smoke hiss, UI and reward cues, and periodic airflow/fan ambience/drone loops.
These layers were generated by tools/build_audio.py. No paid assets or services.

The complete upstream source packs are not needed to play. Shipped clips are
prebuilt in this directory. The developer mastering script requires numpy,
scipy and FFmpeg; players require none of those tools.
