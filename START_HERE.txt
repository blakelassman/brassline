BRASSLINE 0.9.0 - CHALLENGES & LOCKER UPDATE

EVERYONE MUST UPDATE: this build uses a new network protocol.
Update the source checkout or extract a complete package. Keep UDP 27020 and your existing port rule.
Your saved level and settings carry over automatically on the same Windows user.

WHAT CHANGED
- CHALLENGES: 86 permanent milestones, jump shots, collats, 10/30/50 killstreaks.
- Top-center notifications and a quest chime; notifications survive respawning.
- LOCKER: preview and equip operator outfits and all four weapon finishes.
- Open free unlimited cases. Items save automatically. Duplicates stack.
- Special rarity is 0.25% per opening (1 in 400), not a guaranteed 400th case.
- Rifle has a stronger, repeatable 24-round S recoil pattern. Counter it with
  mouse movement; a 650 ms pause resets the pattern. Practice while stationary.

ALSO INCLUDED
- FOV-scaled scope sensitivity; a small amount of counter-strafe momentum.
- Settings: fullscreen, resolution presets and custom width/height. Apply Display.
- Clean HUD. Hold Tab for K/D, levels, ping and FPS.
- Minimap: teammates always; enemies at their last shot position for two seconds.
- Four rebuilt districts with upstairs interiors, smooth stairs and boost roofs.
- Better armor and weapon silhouettes. Sword: left attack, right timed parry.

GIT SOURCE CHECKOUT
Keep your existing engine folder beside project.godot, then run START_BRASSLINE.bat.
Engine binaries are excluded from Git. An existing engine folder survives updates.
Your friends need the same 0.9.0 source and engine folder, or a full portable package.


PLAY
- AIM TRAINING: the original passive targets and movement practice.
- ENDLESS 5v5 BOTS: you + four allies against five bots. No match limit.
- ONLINE TDM: host on your PC; friends connect using your public IP and UDP port.
  First TEAM to 250 kills wins. At 10 minutes, the higher score wins; ties draw.
  A 15-second map vote follows. The chosen arena starts the next round.

HOSTING WITH FRIENDS OUTSIDE YOUR HOME
Open HOSTING.txt. Default port: UDP 27020.
Choose your initial map under PLAY, then ONLINE > HOST & PLAY.
Forward UDP 27020 to your PC and allow the game through Windows Firewall.
Friends enter your PUBLIC IP, the same port, and your optional server password.
They connect to the game server, not to your Wi-Fi network.
You can use another port if you forward that port instead.

TEAMS
Host alone: 1 human + 4 bots versus 5 bots.
First friend: 1 human + 4 bots versus 1 human + 4 bots.
New humans fill opposing teams evenly, up to 5 humans on each team.
Departing humans get bot replacements. Teams rebalance humans if necessary.
Rebalancing moves a player to a fresh spawn and keeps their XP and K/D.
Respawns during play remain instant: no spawn shield and no countdown.

SAVED PROGRESS AND SETTINGS
Your level, XP, name, graphics, sound, sensitivity and controls save locally.
Levels now survive closing and reopening the game. Maximum level: 1000.
The profile belongs to this Windows user on this PC; there is no cloud account.
The previous 0.6 build did not save XP, so closed 0.6 sessions cannot be recovered.

DEFAULT CONTROLS - CHANGE ANY OF THESE IN CONTROLS
WASD: move. Space or scroll down: jump. Ctrl or C: hold crouch.
Mouse left: fire / slash / throw. Mouse right: aim / scope / short grenade toss.
1 rifle. 2 heavy pistol. 3 sword. 4 sniper. R reload.
G equip blast grenade. Q equip smoke. Grenades wait for your throw/toss click.
Tab: hold scoreboard. H: tips. Esc: menu.
F refill and T reset work only in aim training.
Click a control binding, then press a key, mouse button or scroll wheel.
Two bindings per action. A conflicting key moves to the new action.

PERFORMANCE
Start with BALANCED. Try LOW for fewer particles and no falling-body effects.
HIGH adds shadows and anti-aliasing. Frame rate cap and fullscreen are adjustable.
Static map geometry and bot parts now use fewer separate draw calls.
Opening the menu pauses offline practice. Online matches keep running.

OPTIONAL DEDICATED SERVER
START_DEDICATED_SERVER.bat runs a server without a game window.
Edit server.cfg for its port, password and first map. This is optional;
HOST & PLAY is the easiest way to play and host together.

TROUBLESHOOTING
If Windows says the app cannot run, confirm you extracted the entire ZIP
and are on 64-bit Windows. Do not launch the small console EXE by itself.
If the game closes unexpectedly, run RUN_DIAGNOSTICS.bat and send brassline.log.
For connection problems, see the short checklist in HOSTING.txt.
